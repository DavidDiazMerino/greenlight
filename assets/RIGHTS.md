# Demo asset rights and inventory

All Greenlight decision-canary media is generated locally by this repository from simple geometry, gradients, and original short text authored for this project. Submission-edit footage and generated video masters are maintained separately and are not distributed in this repository.

The exact DejaVu Sans font used for deterministic rasterization is vendored at `assets/fonts/DejaVuSans.ttf`. Its upstream copyright and permissive redistribution terms are preserved verbatim in `assets/fonts/DejaVuSans.LICENSE.txt`.

## Source inventory

- `dataset/vertical-social-v1.json`: eight original synthetic motion-card descriptions, caption cues, durations, colors, and locked reframe focus points.
- `src/media.ts`: deterministic SVG construction for 16:9 masters and 9:16 outputs.
- `assets/fonts/`: pinned DejaVu Sans binary, fontconfig boundary, and upstream license.
- `assets/synthetic/generated/`: generated PNG posters and MP4 masters; ignored by Git and reproducible with `make canary`.
- `artifacts/<experiment>/media/`: no-caption, baseline, and candidate portrait renders generated by the canary.

Repository-generated assets are offered under the repository's MIT license. FFmpeg's installed system codecs are used as tools; no codec binaries are redistributed here. Canary audio is intentionally absent.
